# ⚖️ AIA A201 General Conditions & Operational Indemnification Matrix
**Role Profile:** Institutional Construction Risk Manager

## 1. Multi-Tenant Operational Liability Mapping
This project features a unique 51-week to 1-week transformation schedule. Contractual risk is systematically isolated using modified **AIA Document A201-2017** protocols:

* **Construction Phase Liability:** The General Contractor maintains exclusive custody, control, and Builder’s Risk insurance coverage until the formal date of Substantial Completion.
* **Transformation Phase Liability (The 48-Hour Flip):** Damage occurring to flexible partition tracks, automated bulkheads, or high-efficiency curtain glazing during the conversion cycle is contractually shifted to the specialized Event Operations Management firm via a mandatory hold-harmless and indemnification rider.
* **Commercial General Liability (CGL) Caps:** Corporate lab tenants (e.g., FinTech partners) must carry a minimum of $5,000,000 in independent primary CGL coverage, listing the ASU Foundation as an additional insured to prevent institutional exposure to private business losses.
