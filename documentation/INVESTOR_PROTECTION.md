# 📉 Investor Protection & Debt Service Reserve Fund (DSRF) Framework
**Role Profile:** Lead Project Finance Structure Consultant

## 1. Capital Protection Structure
To insulate private investment partners from transient lower-than-average campus occupancy rates, this development implements a structured **Debt Service Reserve Fund (DSRF)**. 

* **The Escrow Buffer:** A minimum of six (6) months of baseline interest and principal debt service will be held in a restricted escrow account.
* **Initial Funding Source:** This account is fully capitalized upfront using the initial $1.5M corporate naming rights liquidity pool provided by regional banking partners.
* **Refill Mechanism:** If the reserve is ever utilized during an academic semester, it is systematically refilled using the high-margin, top-line gross revenues generated during the 1-week Homecoming Alumni Suites event conversion cycle.
