# Section 09 Finish Materials & Durability Specification Sheet
**Role Profile:** Commercial Specifications Writer

## 🏗️ Performance Requirements & Testing Benchmarks

### 1. High-Traffic Flooring System
* **Specification:** Heavy-duty, high-performance bio-polyurethane sheet flooring.
* **Performance Benchmark:** Withstands rolling load weights of up to 2,500 lbs to safely accommodate mobile tracking wall systems and heavy AV staging racks.
* **Testing Standard:** ASTM F970 (Static Load Limit) compliant.

### 2. Flexible Acoustic Partition Wall Tracks
* **Specification:** Ceiling-anchored multi-directional structural aluminum track system with drop-seal panels.
* **Performance Benchmark:** Minimum Sound Transmission Class (STC) rating of 52 to guarantee absolute noise isolation between adjacent alumni suites.
* **Testing Standard:** ASTM E90 (Laboratory Measurement of Airborne Sound Transmission Loss) certified.

### 3. Wall Systems & Protective Finishes
* **Specification:** Rigid, impact-resistant wall cladding panels finished with low-VOC scuff-resistant architectural topcoats.
* **Performance Benchmark:** High-impact resilience against severe hand-truck and mobile furniture collisions during rapid transformation cycles.
* **Testing Standard:** ASTM D6578 (Scuff Resistance) and ASTM D5420 (Impact Resistance) compliant.
