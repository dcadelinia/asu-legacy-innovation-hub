# 🏫 SACSCOC Regional Accreditation & Instructional Alignment
**Role Profile:** Academic Affairs Policy Analyst

## 1. Substantive Change & Facility Integration
The integration of a private corporate incubator stall on the ground floor of a residential facility does not constitute a substantive change to campus infrastructure under SACSCOC guidelines. The facility maintains strict academic control over the site:

* **Educational Integration:** The corporate partnership space functions directly as an applied laboratory for the School of Business Administration.
* **Student Protections:** All corporate lease agreements explicitly mandate the hosting of a minimum of five (5) credit-bearing undergraduate student internships per semester, directly fulfilling [ASU Strategic Objective 3.4 for cultivating business collaborations](https://www.asurams.edu/institutional-effectiveness/strategic-planning/2025.php "Albany State University 2025 Strategic Plan Goals").
* **Environment Control:** Physical lockouts and zero-trust network boundaries guarantee that the student living spaces on the upper floors remain entirely isolated, quiet, and secure academic sanctuaries.
