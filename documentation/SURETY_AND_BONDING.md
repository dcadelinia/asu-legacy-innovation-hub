# 🛡️ Georgia Little Miller Act Performance & Payment Bond Compliance
**Role Profile:** Public Finance & Surety Underwriting Specialist
**Statutory Authority:** O.C.G.A. § 36-91-20 et seq.

## 1. Statutory Bond Protection Metrics
To guarantee absolute completion of the 85,000 sq ft Hub asset without risk to the University System of Georgia or its investment partners, all bidding General Contractors must furnish statutory security bonds:

* **Performance Bond:** Set at exactly 100% of the total accepted contract value (derived from `code/pro_forma.py` metrics) to guarantee the project is executed according to state technical specifications.
* **Payment Bond:** Set at 100% of the total contract value to shield the University from mechanics' liens by protecting all subcontractors, laborers, and material suppliers.
* **Surety Qualification:** All bonds must be underwritten by a surety company possessing an A.M. Best Rating of "A" or better and listed explicitly on the United States Department of the Treasury’s Circular 570.
