# 🏛️ University System of Georgia (USG) Board of Regents Capital Compliance
**Role Profile:** Chief Compliance & State Legal Counsel

## 1. Procurement & Public Bidding Transparency
* **State Requirement:** Any public capital construction or major expansion project matching the Hub's size must comply with the Georgia Procurement Registry requirements and public bidding transparency laws.
* **Our Integration:** The repository includes a `terraform/network_vlan_config.tf` template. This open, infrastructure-as-code setup ensures that any state-contracted IT or civil engineering firm can audit, test, and implement our network core configurations during the open bidding process without proprietary vendor lock-in.

## 2. Environmental & Historical Preservation Reviews
* **State Requirement:** State-funded initiatives must pass environmental impact evaluations and respect local historical designations.
* **Our Integration:** Our design incorporates cross-laminated mass timber systems and active rooftop green spaces. This structure natively satisfies regional stormwater management benchmarks, drastically lowering the building's impact on city infrastructure and streamlining state eco-clearance reviews.

## 3. Mandatory Fiscal & Budgetary Disclosures
* **State Requirement:** State capital project requests must outline precise cost-per-square-foot metrics and prove that auxiliary operations do not misappropriate public education funds.
* **Our Integration:** The validation pipeline (`code/pro_forma.py` and `code/homecoming_revenue_capture.py`) separates student auxiliary revenue from corporate and alumni event monetization channels. This distinct tracking prevents public education funds from cross-contaminating private development streams, matching USG fiscal transparency mandates perfectly.
